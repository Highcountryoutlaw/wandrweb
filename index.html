Stand With Kelsey

When family needs us most, community can make the weight a little lighter. This page is a place for friends, kind strangers, and the WANDR community to offer support while Kelsey and her loved ones spend precious time with Shannon in hospice.

Why We’re Here 🌲

Kelsey and her family are navigating an incredibly difficult chapter as Shannon receives hospice care. Being present with loved ones can bring unexpected costs and practical challenges—meals, transportation, everyday essentials, and time away from normal responsibilities.

This community effort is meant to offer a little breathing room and remind Kelsey that she does not have to face every burden alone. Every contribution, share, encouraging message, or act of kindness matters.

Support is offered with love, dignity, and no expectation.

🍲 Everyday Needs

Help with groceries, meals, and essentials during a stressful time.

🚗 Getting There

Support transportation, fuel, and the practical costs of being present with family.

💜 A Little Breathing Room

Help ease immediate financial pressure so family can focus on time, care, and each other.
